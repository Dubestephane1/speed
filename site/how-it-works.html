How it works

No redesign. No risk. No downtime. Just numbers you can verify yourself.

1. The test

You enter your URL. I run the same mobile speed test Google itself publishes — pagespeed.web.dev — and you get two numbers you can check yourself:

  • Your performance score (0–100). Below 50 is red, 50–89 is orange, 90+ is green.
  • Your load time (LCP — the seconds before your main content appears on a phone).

The goal is green (90+) with LCP under 2.5 seconds. Most local business sites I test sit at 10–44 out of 100 with 8–23 second load times.

2. The fix list

You get the exact list of what's slow, in plain language:

  • Images — oversized photos loading at full resolution on phones.
  • Scripts — plugins and trackers blocking the page from rendering.
  • Server settings — slow response, missing caching, and compression off.

Each fix comes with the load time you can expect after it's applied. No redesign, no risk, no downtime — your site keeps working exactly as it is.

3. The re-test

After the fixes, you re-run the same public Google test. You see the difference yourself — same tool, same numbers, same page. I don't ask you to take my word for it; the scoreboard is public.
