Canadian website speed by city: the 2026 ranking

September 24, 2026 · 5 min read

I audit Canadian business websites for free at speed.stephanedube.dev. So far: 650 sites — dental clinics, heating and air-conditioning companies, real estate agents, optometrists, and physiotherapy clinics across the country.

Most cities would love to know where they stand. But honest rankings need real sample sizes, so here's what I'm confident enough to publish: the 20 cities where I audited at least 10 websites, ranked fastest to slowest.

The story the numbers tell

  • Gatineau tops the table — all dentists, and the only city where less than a fifth of sites fail (12%).
  • Windsor is last: every single site fails, and its worst audit scores 12/100. Calgary matches it with a 100% failure rate.
  • Toronto is the big story. This ranking originally showed Toronto near the top — until I pulled in its dentists, whose scores had been left out of the count. With 19 dental clinics included, Toronto drops to 47/100 with 69% of sites failing. The city's dentists alone average 42/100.
  • Vancouver's worst site scored 6/100 — the lowest in the entire 650-site study.
  • Ottawa is a tale of two cities: the best audit in the whole ranking (99/100) and an 89% failure rate.
  • Edmonton fails at 94%, Lévis at 90% — the bottom half of this table is painful reading.
  • Across all 650 sites nationwide, only 24 reach green (90+). Winnipeg owns the only green site in the top half of this table (90/100).

An earlier version of this ranking published Toronto at 62/100 — the top of the table. That was wrong: the scores for Toronto's dentists had not been merged into the city count. This version uses the complete audit record (650 sites), and Toronto now sits where the data actually puts it. If your city's numbers look off, tell me — real data gets fixed, and this page gets updated.

The honest fine print

This is combined mode: every niche I audited in each city is pooled together. Samples differ city to city — Gatineau is all dentists, Calgary is all dentists plus HVAC — which is why the next post in this series compares industries head to head across Canada. Every site is anonymous.

It's a convenience sample of real audited businesses, not a census, and scores shift as sites change. I only rank cities with 10+ audits — fewer than that isn't reliable enough to publish. (That's why most of the country isn't ranked yet: those cities need more audits.)
