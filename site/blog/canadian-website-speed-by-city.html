Canadian website speed by city: the 2026 ranking

September 24, 2026 · 4 min read

I audit Canadian business websites for free at speed.stephanedube.dev. So far: 544 sites across 45 cities — dental clinics, heating and air-conditioning companies, real estate agents, and optometrists from Vancouver to St. John's.

Most cities would love to know where they stand. But honest rankings need real sample sizes, so here's what I'm confident enough to publish: the 10 cities where I audited at least 10 websites, ranked worst to best.

The leaderboard

Average mobile performance score, 0–100. Under 50 is the "red" danger zone on Google's own scale; 90+ is green. Here's the whole ranking:

  1. Toronto — 62/100 · 13 sites · 23% failing · best 79, worst 33
  2. Gatineau — 61/100 · 16 sites · 12% failing · best 97, worst 27
  3. Longueuil — 57/100 · 24 sites · 29% failing · best 91, worst 23
  4. Mississauga — 57/100 · 51 sites · 33% failing · best 98, worst 24
  5. Québec City — 57/100 · 53 sites · 30% failing · best 89, worst 26
  6. Victoria — 55/100 · 10 sites · 40% failing · best 82, worst 26
  7. Ottawa — 55/100 · 11 sites · 45% failing · best 99, worst 19
  8. Edmonton — 47/100 · 10 sites · 70% failing · best 84, worst 16
  9. Vancouver — 46/100 · 13 sites · 54% failing · best 82, worst 8
  10. Lévis — 36/100 · 10 sites · 90% failing · best 61, worst 19

The story the numbers tell

  • Toronto, the biggest city, has the fastest websites. The headline nobody expects — and worth more data before anyone celebrates.
  • Lévis is last by a mile: 9 of its 10 audited sites fail the 50-point bar. The lowest average I've recorded in any city with 10+ sites.
  • Gatineau has the cleanest scorecard — only 12% failing, and a best score of 97.
  • Ottawa is a tale of two cities: it owns the single best audit in the whole ranking (99/100) and a 45% failure rate.
  • Vancouver's worst site scored 8/100 — the lowest in my entire 544-site study.
  • Only 4 of the 10 cities have any green (90+) site at all. The national average is 57 — half of these cities sit below it.

The honest fine print

This is combined mode: every niche I audited in each city is pooled together. Samples differ city to city — Gatineau is all dentists, Ottawa leans HVAC — which is why the next post in this series compares industries head to head across Canada. Every site is anonymous.

It's a convenience sample of real audited businesses, not a census, and scores shift as sites change. I only rank cities with 10+ audits — fewer than that isn't reliable enough to publish. (That's why 35 of the 45 cities aren't here yet: they need more audits.)
